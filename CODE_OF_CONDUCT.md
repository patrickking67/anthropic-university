# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Anthropic University to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to learn and contribute,
regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Assume good faith and give helpful feedback on contributions.
- Keep content **accurate** and **original** — no real/leaked certification exam questions.
- Respect that this is an **unofficial** community project, not affiliated with Anthropic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing confidential exam content or encouraging exam misconduct.
- Spam, plagiarism, or deliberately misleading study material.

## Reporting

Open a private report by contacting the repository owner via GitHub. Maintainers may remove content
or contributors that violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
